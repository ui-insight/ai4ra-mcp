# Award Status

Reads an award's status from an Award status sheet: the award's own accounts listed from a Lines sheet (a Banner FRIGITD export) with budget, spent and committed under each, flags for accounts out of step with the time elapsed, burn rate, runway, the projected spend at the end date, an F&A check against the rate, the rebudget each pool's pace implies, and the closeout figures. Ships a template with every figure a formula; the skill writes nothing.

**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Category:** post-award · **Status:** experimental · **Output:** a spreadsheet sheet

## Inputs

The award's title, sponsor and number, PI, start and end dates, and a Lines sheet from the award-lines skill. The F&A rate is 50% in the template; the award's own rate is typed in Excel.

## Outputs

The Award status sheet (template.json, regenerated by make_template.py) and a short report of what it shows.
