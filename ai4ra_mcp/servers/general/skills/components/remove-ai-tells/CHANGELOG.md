# Changelog

All notable changes to this component. Versions follow semver: MAJOR for output-contract breaks, MINOR for backward-compatible additions, PATCH for wording or clarity.

## [0.3.1] — 2026-09-14

- Edits in logical blocks: one paragraph per tracked change, or one sentence via the replace tool's new find parameter; never a span of paragraphs.

## [0.3.0] — 2026-09-14

- Twenty-nine tell families in five groups, merged from Wikipedia's catalogue and four 2026 editors' lists, including emoji bullets (✅ ❌), Latinate substitutions, participle openers, from-X-to-Y sweeps, personified abstractions, hedge stacking, the challenges formula, canned notability, tiny tables and leaked Markdown; a grammar-only pass at the end, reported separately.

## [0.2.1] — 2026-09-14

- Back to the pane's normal tracked edits: the skill no longer switches the document's Track Changes setting and adds no comments.

## [0.2.0] — 2026-09-14

- Turns the document's Track Changes on before editing and adds no comments; 0.1.1's per-change comments are gone.

## [0.1.1] — 2026-09-14

- A comment on every large change (deleted sentence, cut claim, heavily reworded paragraph) saying which family was found and what was done, with the original wording of anything cut.

## [0.1.0] — 2026-09-14

- Initial version: ten tell families from Wikipedia's catalogue, tracked-change edits in Word, report without accusation.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-16

- Two passes: read and mark every paragraph's families first, then rewrite by paragraph, one replace call per paragraph, with find kept for a single word. The sentence-by-sentence rule had missed the families that span sentences and stalled on failed finds. The 29 families are the same, condensed to a checklist; the prompt is half its length.
