# PI Memo

Assembles the email to the principal investigator from the sentences the Award status sheet builds by formula (opener, flagged accounts, charges with no budget line, runway, F&A, pace against budget), facts only,, labelled Subject and Body in column A with their text in column B, ready to paste. Copying only; a run that interpreted the figures called a needed approval an approved one.

**Version:** 2.1.0 · **Category:** post-award · **Status:** experimental · **Output:** a spreadsheet sheet

## Inputs

An Award status sheet (award-status skill) and the PI's name.

## Outputs

A Memo sheet: A1 Subject and B1 the subject, A2 Body and B2 the body with line breaks.
