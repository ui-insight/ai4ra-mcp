# Changelog

All notable changes to this component. Versions follow semver: MAJOR for output-contract breaks, MINOR for backward-compatible additions, PATCH for wording or clarity.

## [0.1.0] — 2026-09-14

- Initial version: narrative and/or announcement in, questions with defaults, worksheet out with formulas for every derived cell and yellow fill on every estimate.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-14

- Renamed from narrative-to-budget-worksheet and reduced to one job: fill the NSF template from a Budget outline sheet. The questions and the narrative reading moved to the budget-outline skill.

## [2.0.0] — 2026-09-15

- Template regenerated by make_template.py: typed columns A to E for people and typed rows 29 to 39 for the other lines are contiguous, formulas follow them, so a person is one write and the lines are one write. Senior people are capped at two months in the expansion.

## [3.0.0] — 2026-09-15

- The pane fills every non-personnel line from the outline's category amounts; the people are left for the research administrator, with the outline's personnel amounts shown as the target in row 27. The skill only reads back and reports. Months per year are typed again.

## [3.0.1] — 2026-09-15

- The personnel rows are locked to the model's writes (the administrator types them in Excel) and the skill writes nothing: a run overwrote the PI's salary with the outline's amount.

## [3.1.0] — 2026-09-15

- No derived rows: trip counts, the student count and tuition-from-count are gone, with their unit-cost inputs. Travel is one typed line and tuition a typed line the administrator fills. Rows renumber (people 19-24, targets 25, lines 27-36, NSF lines 39-58, checks 60-62). The prompt no longer names the pane.

## [3.2.0] — 2026-09-22

- Institution-agnostic: the template ships no rates (0, origin "not set") and the skill fills B8:B13 from a sheet named Rates when the workbook has one, writing its Source line into D12 as provenance; the University of Idaho figures moved to the uidaho-rates-sheet skill. Moved to ui-insight/ai4ra-mcp as a skill of the ai4ra server.
